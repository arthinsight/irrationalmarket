Arth Insight

Indian Stock Market Data and Intelligence

Arth Insight is an Indian stock-market data and intelligence platform. It brings together public filings, exchange disclosures and market data for 2,660 listed Indian companies, and presents them in a form investors can actually read.

What Arth Insight covers

  • IPOs — prospectus-sourced company reference for new and recently listed issues, anchor and lead-manager track records, post-listing performance and lock-in calendars
  • Corporate announcements — a daily digest of what companies filed with the exchanges, with the material events separated from the routine
  • Company financials — quarterly results and financial history
  • Momentum and themes — where price and participation are concentrating across the market

How it is built

Arth Insight reads primary sources — company filings with NSE and BSE, offer documents, and exchange circulars — and states what they say, with the figure and the document it came from. Where a number is derived, it is labelled as derived.

What Arth Insight is not

Arth Insight is an educational and informational platform. It does not provide investment advice, stock recommendations, price targets or portfolio services, and nothing on this site should be read as a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Observe only. Please consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser before making investment decisions.
